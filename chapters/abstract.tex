\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

DePaso is a collaborative urban logistics digital platform for the Buenos Aires Metropolitan Area (AMBA), connecting senders ---SMEs, businesses, and individuals--- with carriers willing to deliver packages along routes they already travel regularly. The project introduces a hybrid model that combines dedicated transport with collaborative transport, leveraging idle vehicle capacity to reduce costs, CO\textsubscript{2} emissions, and delivery times.

The platform comprises four main technical components: (1)~a computer vision load classifier that determines package type and size from a photograph; (2)~a multivariable assignment algorithm that weights geospatial compatibility, route deviation, load type, carrier reputation, and time window; (3)~real-time shipment tracking; and (4)~a CO\textsubscript{2} savings calculator benchmarked against dedicated transport, using IPCC emission factors.

{\color{nuevo}This document corresponds to the 50\% milestone of the Final Engineering Project. It presents the theoretical framework underpinning the design decisions, the state of the art and the competitive analysis, the results of the user research (a survey with 145 responses from AMBA residents and workers, complemented by semi-structured interviews), the requirements analysis with its use cases, and the development methodology: architecture, technologies, data model and system validation. Quantitative findings show high willingness to use collaborative shipping (69.8\%), a strong preference for price transparency (75\% cite cost as the primary criterion), and an overlap between senders' willingness to pay (ARS~3,000--6,000 for a small package) and carriers' minimum acceptable compensation (ARS~2,500--5,000), a preliminary compatibility between both sides of the market whose translation into economic feasibility remains to be confirmed by the cost analysis planned for the next stage.}

\textbf{Keywords:} collaborative logistics, crowdsourced delivery, last-mile logistics, two-sided markets, computer vision, AMBA.
